OSINT, IN ONE MINUTE.

Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) is the process of finding, collecting, connecting and verifying information that is publicly available.

PUBLIC WEB
The starting point. Search engines, archives, and open databases.
IMAGE
Visual evidence reveals location, time and context.
PROFILE
Digital footprints, usernames, and public activity.
DOCUMENT
Public records, metadata, and archived text.
VERIFICATION
Cross-referencing clues to confirm factual accuracy.
INTELLIGENCE
Actionable, verified information derived from public sources.
